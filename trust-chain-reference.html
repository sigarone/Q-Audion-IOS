Protection starts at the seal. ML-KEM-1024 + AES-256, keys locked in TrustZone — but the microphone sits just outside the protected zone: raw audio is captured in the phone for the instant before it's sealed, so a compromised OS could tap the mic upstream of encryption, without ever needing the key.

Protection now extends all the way to the microphone. The mic and the seal both live inside the earbud's secure chip, so your voice is protected at the source and never crosses into the phone. Nothing raw is left outside the envelope — even a fully compromised phone only ever handles ciphertext.
